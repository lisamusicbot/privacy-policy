# Lisa Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** October 09, 2026<br>
**Last Updated:** October 09, 2026

Thank you for choosing **Lisa**! This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your data when you use our service. By using Lisa, you agree to the terms outlined in this policy.


## 1. Data Collection

Lisa is a free-to-use Discord music bot with optional premium features. To provide and improve our services, we collect only the data necessary for the bot to function, including:

* **Server IDs:** Used to store server-specific settings such as player configuration, autoplay, DJ roles, 24/7 mode, default volume, and other music preferences.
* **User IDs:** Used for premium access, bot interactions, and feature-related functionality.
* **Channel IDs:** Used to remember configured music channels and player channels.
* **Voice Channel IDs:** Used for voice connection management and music playback.
* **Premium Information:** Stored only to verify and manage Premium subscriptions.

All collected data is securely stored and is used solely for providing Lisa's features.


## 2. How We Use Your Data

We do **not** sell, share, rent, or distribute your information to third parties.

Your data is used only to:

* Provide music playback and bot functionality.
* Store server-specific preferences.
* Verify Premium subscriptions.
* Improve performance, reliability, and user experience.
* Detect abuse, bugs, or malicious activity.

All data usage complies with our **Terms of Service**.


## 3. Access to Data

* **Server Owners & Administrators** control the bot's settings within their servers.
* **Bot Developers** may access stored data only when necessary for maintenance, debugging, security, or service improvements.
* Developers **cannot** access your Discord account credentials, passwords, or private messages.


## 4. Data Storage & Retention

Lisa stores only the information required for its features to function.

When the bot is removed from a server, server-related configuration data may be retained for **up to 30 days** to allow easy restoration if the bot is re-invited.

After this period, eligible data may be permanently deleted.

Users may request data deletion at any time through our Support Server.


## 5. Third-Party Services

Lisa may interact with third-party services such as Discord, Lavalink nodes, Spotify, YouTube, Apple Music, Deezer, or other supported platforms to provide music playback.

These services have their own privacy policies, and Grooky is not responsible for how they collect or process data.


## 6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect stored data from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure.

While we strive to keep your information secure, no online service can guarantee absolute security.


## 7. Children's Privacy

Lisa is intended for users who comply with Discord's minimum age requirements. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.


## 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time.

Continued use of Lisa after any changes indicates your acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.


## 9. Contact & Support

For questions, concerns, or data deletion requests, please join our **[Support Server](https://discord.gg/7mqbTxjbJp)**.


Thank you for trusting **Lisa**. We are committed to protecting your privacy while providing a secure, reliable, and enjoyable music experience.
